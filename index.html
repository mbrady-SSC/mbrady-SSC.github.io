About

From the dining room to the design table.

I'm Matthew Brady, a Kansas City storyteller who writes young adult fantasy in his off hours and is building a career in technology. Twenty-five years in customer service taught me that everyone matters: everyone has a story, and everyone wants to be heard.

I learned to listen, to adjust, and to exceed expectations. I learned that people are funny, kind, talented, and smart, and that they have so much to give when you know how to receive it.

Today I bring that to technology, with a Cisco CCST certification, seven AI credentials, and hands-on user experience design through the i.c.stars program. My goal is to help teams adopt AI, and to teach them to use it as both a thought and work partner.
