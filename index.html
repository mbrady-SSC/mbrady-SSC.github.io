About Me

From hospitality to technology

I'm Matthew Brady, and I'm building a career in technology and artificial intelligence. Before tech, I spent many years in fine dining, hospitality, and event planning, including about ten years with Darden Restaurant Group as a Shift Manager, Corporate Trainer, and Private Functions Coordinator.

That work taught me how to stay calm under pressure, explain things clearly, and take care of the people in front of me. I bring those same skills to technology. I hold the Cisco Certified Support Technician (CCST) certification, along with seven AI credentials from IBM SkillsBuild and Google, and I'm currently growing my skills through the i.c.stars technology program in Kansas City.

Outside of work, I write young adult fantasy.

The Moonwater Locket

Young Adult Fantasy

In a kingdom where magic carries a death sentence, Aelyn keeps her pointed ears hidden beneath a kerchief and her elven heritage a secret. When a locket tied to her lost lineage awakens, it draws her into the Elderwood and toward a truth she was never meant to find.

Told in two voices, the novel follows Aelyn and Gwynn, a street-smart thief whose search through old archives turns up records she would rather not have found.

Then the reflection blinked.
Aelyn did not.
